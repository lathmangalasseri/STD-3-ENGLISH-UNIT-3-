English - Standard 3

Unit 3: In Harmony Max Marks: 20
I. Read the passage and answer the questions. (4 Marks)
Rocky saw a cat chasing a squirrel. He thought of having some fun. He chased the cat. "Leave me, let me catch the squirrel," the cat hissed at him. By then, the squirrel had climbed up a tree. "Chill, Chill, Chill," it mocked the cat.
1. Who did Rocky see chasing a squirrel?
2. What did Rocky think of doing?
3. What did the cat say to Rocky?
4. Pick out an action word (verb) from the passage.
II. Write True or False. (3 Marks)
5. The crow snatched the bone from Rocky. [ _____ ]
6. Rocky saved the mother crow. [ _____ ]
7. The wind and the sun were fighting about their colour. [ _____ ]
III. Match the animal with its young one. (3 Marks)
A. Dog1. Calf
B. Cow2. Owlet
C. Owl3. Puppy
IV. Complete the sequence of events. (5 Marks)
  1. The wind and the sun argued about their strength.
  2. They saw a man walking with a shirt on.
  4. The sun started blazing.
V. Creative Writing. (5 Marks)
10. Rocky saved the mother crow and her chicks. Write Rocky's thoughts after helping them.
Answer Key:
1. A cat.
2. Having some fun and chasing the cat.
3. "Leave me, let me catch the squirrel."
4. saw / chasing / thought / chased / hissed / climbed / mocked.
5. True.
6. True.
7. False (They argued about their strength).
Match: A-3, B-1, C-2.
Sequence missing parts (Examples):
- The wind tried to blow away the man's shirt, but he held it tight.
- The man felt hot and took off his shirt. The sun won.
Thoughts: Should express happiness and relief about saving the crow, even though she snatched his bone earlier. Kindness is good.
